Privacy policy for Bee Voice Notes

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Bee Voice Notes is a personal tool used only by its owner, the owner of this GitHub account. It is not available to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Google Tasks lists and adds new tasks to them. It doesn't edit, complete or delete tasks, and it doesn't access any other Google data or anyone else's data.

How the data is used

New task titles and due dates come from the owner's own voice notes. An AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), running on the owner's computer, reads each note and the owner's open tasks to decide what to add. The data is used only to provide this tool's features to its owner. It isn't sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Where it is stored

The Google sign-in is kept only in the keychain of the owner's own computer. A short record of the tasks the tool added is kept on that computer. Nothing is stored on this website.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
