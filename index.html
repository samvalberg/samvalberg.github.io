Bee Voice Notes

Bee Voice Notes is a personal tool, built and used by one person: the owner of this GitHub account. It turns the voice notes they record on their Bee wearable into tasks in their own Google Tasks.

It is not offered to anyone else. There is nothing to sign up for, download or install here, and it has no other users.

With its owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Google Tasks lists and adds new tasks to them. It never edits, completes or deletes tasks, and it doesn't access anyone else's data.
